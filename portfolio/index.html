Who Is She

Hi, I'm Marcela. American and Peruvian, and right now I'm running the Peruvian half.

I grew up in the US, went to school there, and started working there. Urgent care front desk, then a Wawa register. In 2025 I moved to Lima. Now I work US hours for US companies from here, which turns out to be the most natural thing in the world when you already belong in both places.

The industry changes every time. Urgent care, then roofing crews, now talent and operations for a company that places executive assistants. The job underneath never changes. Hold every detail, follow every thread, write it down so the next person picks it up without asking.

I build things too. CRMs, screening tools, SOP databases, websites, this one included. Mostly self taught, mostly with AI, mostly because I needed the thing to exist.

The Zeno Files

Talent and Operations Lead. May 2026 to now.

Zeno Worlds places AI-fluent executive assistants from Latin America with US business owners. I own the talent side end to end.

I build the candidate pipelines. I run first-round interviews and turn them into structured overviews, so leadership reads one clean page instead of six resumes. I keep the CRM accurate, profiles, statuses, documents, all of it. I write the SOPs for recruiting, onboarding, and internal operations, then keep them current when the process changes. I handle onboarding logistics for new hires. I follow up with clients and run discovery calls.
